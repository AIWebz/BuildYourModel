The little robot woke up early in the morning.
The little robot looked out of the window and saw the sun.
The sun was bright and the sky was blue.
The robot wanted to explore the town.
The robot walked down the street and said hello to the baker.
The baker gave the robot a warm loaf of bread.
The robot did not eat bread, but it said thank you.
The robot walked to the park and sat under a tall tree.
A small bird landed on the robot and sang a song.
The robot listened to the song and felt happy.
The robot wanted to learn the song.
The bird sang the song again, slowly this time.
The robot repeated the song and the bird was surprised.
The bird and the robot became good friends.
Every morning the bird visited the robot in the park.
One day the bird did not come to the park.
The robot was worried and went to look for the bird.
The robot searched the town and asked the baker for help.
The baker said the bird was near the old bridge.
The robot walked to the old bridge as fast as it could.
The bird was stuck in a net near the river.
The robot carefully cut the net and freed the bird.
The bird sang a happy song and flew around the robot.
The robot and the bird walked home together.
That night the robot looked at the stars.
The stars were bright and the moon was full.
The robot thought about the bird and the baker and the town.
The robot was glad to have friends.
The next morning the robot woke up early again.
The robot went to the bakery and helped the baker carry flour.
The baker smiled and said the robot was a good helper.
The robot learned how to bake bread from the baker.
The robot baked a loaf of bread for the bird.
The bird ate the crumbs and sang a new song.
The robot learned the new song too.
Soon the whole town knew the songs of the robot and the bird.
Children came to the park to hear the robot sing.
The robot sang every afternoon under the tall tree.
The town was happy, and the robot was happy too.
The little robot had found a home.
