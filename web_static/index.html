What can Solen build?
  • Text classifiers: sentiment, spam, topics
  • Number predictors: prices, scores
  • Table classifiers: which category a row is
  • Clustering: find groups in your data
  • Anomaly detectors: spot unusual rows
  • Forecasters: predict the next values
  • Recommenders: people who liked X also liked Y
  • Text generators: n-gram and neural character models
  • Image classifiers: small pictures
  • Game agents: reinforcement learning in a maze

Every model is small, trained from scratch, and scored on held-out data.

Solen 1.0 runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
